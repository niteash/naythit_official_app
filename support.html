Support

For account, billing, source, notification or audio help, email swa.developer.expert@gmail.com. Never send a password, access token or payment-card details.

Request account deletion

In Nay Thit, open Settings → Account and security → Delete account, enter the displayed confirmation and submit the request.

If you cannot access the app, email swa.developer.expert@gmail.com from the address associated with your Nay Thit account. Use the subject “Nay Thit Account Deletion.” We may need to verify account ownership, but we will never ask for your password.

An accepted request blocks further account use and schedules deletion of the authentication account, profile, preferences, saved articles, listening activity, generated briefings, private audio, private profile images and notification tokens. Shared news, categories and publisher records are not user-owned and are not deleted. Limited operational records may remain after the account identifier is removed, and provider backups or security logs follow their own retention cycles.

If you have a Google Play subscription, cancel it separately in Google Play to stop future renewal. Deleting the Nay Thit account does not itself control Google Play billing.
