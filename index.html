01 / Overview

Nay Thit ("we", "us", "our") is a mobile news and audio briefing application. This policy describes how information is processed when you use the app, interact with its content or contact the developer.

This policy applies to the Nay Thit mobile application and its related website. At the date above, live advertising is disabled by default in production configuration. Android development builds may display Google's test ads after the consent flow permits an ad request.

02 / Information we process

  • Account and sign-in details: Supabase Auth processes your email address, account identifier and sign-in credentials. You may sign in with Google or with an email and password. Nay Thit does not receive your Google password or a readable copy of your account password.
  • Optional profile details: display name, international-format mobile number and a profile image. The mobile number is not used for OTP sign-in.
  • Preferences: selected news categories and settings such as delivery time and timezone, briefing length, notification choices, data-saving options, crypto watchlist and optional horoscope sign.
  • News and listening activity: saved articles, story feedback, briefing requests, generated scripts, audio records, source references, listening progress and reports submitted about summaries or pronunciation.
  • Device and notification information: an Android Firebase Cloud Messaging token is stored when you opt in to notifications. The app also keeps an authenticated session, preferences and any downloaded audio in app-private storage on your device.
  • Purchase information: RevenueCat and the app store process subscription and entitlement information. Nay Thit does not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Advertising and consent information: Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks whether a consent or privacy-options message is required on Android. If an ad is requested, Google AdMob and its partners may process IP address, device or advertising identifiers, consent choices and ad impressions or interactions as permitted by your settings and applicable law.
  • Optional communications: information you send when requesting support or exercising privacy rights.
  • Technical request information: the API and website hosting providers receive ordinary request data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps and error information in operational logs.

Some information is provided directly by you; other information is generated when the app and its service providers operate.

03 / How information is used

We process information to sign users in, maintain profiles, retrieve and personalize news, generate or deliver audio briefings, remember preferences, protect the app from misuse, provide purchases and notifications, respond to support requests, and improve reliability. If advertising is enabled, relevant data may also be processed to display and measure ads and prevent advertising fraud.

Nay Thit does not currently include a separate product analytics, session-recording or crash-reporting SDK. The landing website source does not set cookies or load an analytics script. Hosting, authentication, advertising and other providers may still create their own operational records as described here and in their policies.

04 / Third-party services

  • Supabase: authentication, database records, private profile images and private briefing-audio storage.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, UMP and AdMob, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Google Play services.
  • RevenueCat: subscription entitlement verification and signed billing events.
  • Google Gemini: generation of grounded briefing text from selected article and source material.
  • Microsoft Azure AI Speech: conversion of the generated script into Burmese audio.
  • Railway: hosting and operational logs for the Nay Thit API.
  • Vercel: hosting and ordinary request logs for this landing website.

Gemini receives selected article titles, descriptions, source material and category context; the prompt is not designed to include your account identifier. Azure receives the generated narration text. Opening an original article sends a request to that publisher under the publisher's own privacy terms.

05 / Advertising and consent

The Android app integrates Google UMP and AdMob. UMP checks at app startup whether Google requires a consent form or privacy options for the current user. The AdMob SDK is initialized only after Google reports that ads may be requested and the build is configured to show ads.

During development, Google test advertisements may appear. A test advertisement is not monetized. Live production ad requests remain disabled unless the production build explicitly enables them and supplies a production banner ad-unit ID.

When Google requires it, a Privacy Choices control is available in Settings so you can reopen Google's privacy-options form. Device-level controls may also limit or reset advertising identifiers. A consent choice does not prevent processing that a provider is legally permitted to perform for security, fraud prevention or basic service operation.

For more on Google's use of information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

06 / Your choices and rights

You can manage available news preferences and profile fields within the app, remove your profile image, manage notification permission in Android settings and remove downloaded audio from the app. Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may also request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information.

To request account deletion in the app, open Settings, choose Account and security, choose Delete account and enter the shown confirmation. An accepted request immediately blocks further account use and deactivates registered notification tokens. The server then schedules removal of the authentication account, account-linked database records, stored briefing audio and private profile images. Processing may be asynchronous, but the accepted deletion marker immediately blocks further account use.

If you cannot access the app, contact swa.developer.expert@gmail.com. Never send a password. We may need to verify account ownership.

07 / Retention and security

We apply access controls and other safeguards intended to protect the information used by Nay Thit. No online service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information for as long as needed to operate account features, preserve briefing history, meet legitimate security and billing needs, and comply with applicable legal requirements. Terminal generation and notification records are designed for bounded cleanup.

The deletion workflow is designed to verify removal of private briefing audio, private profile images and current account-linked application records before it marks deletion as complete. Operational billing-event records may be retained for a bounded period after the account identifier is removed. Provider security logs and backups may follow the provider's own retention cycle and cannot always be removed from an existing backup immediately.

Nay Thit and its service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Those locations may have different data-protection rules. Contact us for questions about providers, retention or an account-deletion request.

08 / Children's privacy

Nay Thit is a general-interest news product and is not designed specifically for children. The current app does not include an age-verification flow. It must not be listed as child-directed or enabled for child-directed advertising without a separate legal, store-policy and technical review.

09 / Contact and updates

For privacy questions or data rights requests, contact swa.developer.expert@gmail.com.

We may update this policy as Nay Thit changes. We will publish the effective date and update this page to reflect material changes in information practices.
